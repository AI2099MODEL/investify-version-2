# Privacy Policy for StockBreak

**Effective Date:** August 7, 2026

**StockBreak** ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data of our users ("you" or "user"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application **StockBreak** (Package: `com.aistudio.stockanalysis.bxkjqw`).

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information Provided by You
- **Watchlists & Market Preferences:** Stock ticker symbols, watchlist items, and preferences you set inside the app. These are stored locally on your device.
- **Support Requests:** If you contact us via email, we receive your email address and any details provided in your query.

### B. Automatically Collected Information
- **App Usage & Performance Data:** Diagnostics, crash logs, and performance metrics (via standard Google Play Services) to ensure app stability.
- **Device Information:** Non-identifiable device details such as OS version, app version, and language settings to deliver formatted financial charts and data.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information solely to:
- Provide real-time and historical stock market analysis and charting.
- Save your custom stock watchlists and preferences locally on your device.
- Maintain, optimize, and improve app stability and performance.
- Respond to customer support inquiries.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

**StockBreak** does not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. We may use standard third-party services that process minimal data governed by their respective privacy policies:

- **Google Play Services:** Used for app distribution, updates, and core system APIs. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Financial Market Data APIs:** Public stock market data requests (do not transmit personal identifiers).

---

## 4. Data Security & Local Storage

- Your stock watchlists, analysis history, and app preferences are stored **locally on your device**.
- We implement standard security measures to protect data during transmission (HTTPS/TLS for market data queries).

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

**StockBreak** is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

---

## 6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

Since your preferences and watchlist data are stored locally on your device, you can clear this data at any time by:
1. Clearing the app storage/cache in your Android device settings.
2. Uninstalling the app.

For any privacy-related inquiries, you can reach out to us directly.
