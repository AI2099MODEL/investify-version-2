## Data & Account Deletion Policy

At **StockBreak**, we value user privacy and transparency. Whether you created an account or used the application as a guest, you have full control over your personal data and account deletion.

### 1. How to Request Account & Data Deletion

You can request the permanent deletion of your account and any associated data through either of the following methods:

#### Method A: Direct In-App Data Clearance (Instant)
Since primary user preferences, watchlists, and local configurations are stored directly on your device:
1. Open **Settings** on your Android device.
2. Go to **Apps** > **StockBreak**.
3. Tap **Storage & Cache** > **Clear Data** and **Clear Cache**.
4. Uninstall the app from your device.

#### Method B: Submit an Email Deletion Request
To request deletion of your account credentials, profile details, or any cloud/support records:
1. Send an email to **nitinjain2099@gmail.com** with the subject: `Account & Data Deletion Request - StockBreak`.
2. Include your registered email address or account username.
3. Our privacy team will process your request and delete all records within **30 days**.
